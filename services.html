Our services

Bespoke joinery and complete home improvements — one trusted team for the whole job.

What we offer

Everything for your home, done properly.

Whether it's a single fitted wardrobe or a full extension, we bring the craftsmanship, the trades and the project management together — so you deal with one team from start to finish.

Bespoke Joinery

Fitted wardrobes, alcove units, shelving, cupboards, media walls, staircases and one-off pieces — designed and made to measure for your space.

Kitchens & Bathrooms

Full kitchen and bathroom projects — design, supply and installation, including units, worktops, tiling, plumbing and finishing touches.

Extensions

Single-storey extensions and rear returns that add real living space and value — managed from drawings and approvals through to finish.

Garage Conversions

Convert an underused garage into a warm, usable room — home office, gym, playroom, snug or utility.

Windows & Doors

Supply and fitting of windows, internal and external doors — improving warmth, security and the look of your home.

Fencing & Decking

Garden fencing, gates and timber decking, built solidly to stand up to the Scottish weather.

Flooring

Laminate, engineered and solid wood flooring supplied and fitted to a high standard.

Structural & Wall Removal

Removing walls, forming openings and structural alterations — carried out safely and to specification.

Exterior Wood Panelling

Feature cladding and exterior timber panelling to lift the look of your home's frontage.
