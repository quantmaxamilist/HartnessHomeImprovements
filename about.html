About Hartness

A local, Strathaven-based joinery and home-improvement team — built on craftsmanship and doing things properly.

Who we are

Led by Lewis, backed by a trusted team.

Hartness Home Improvements Limited is a Strathaven-based joinery and home-improvement company covering South Lanarkshire and the surrounding areas — Hamilton, East Kilbride, Lanark, Motherwell, Larkhall and Biggar.

Led by Lewis — described by clients as "undoubtedly a perfectionist and extremely hard working" — our in-house team and trusted trades cover every part of your project, from bespoke joinery to full renovations. One point of contact, one accountable team, one high standard.

Accredited & verified

Constructionline Gold Members.

We're an independently verified Constructionline Gold member — checked for health & safety, insurance and financial standing. It's third-party proof that we run a professional, reliable and fully insured business.
